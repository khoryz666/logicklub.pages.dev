About This Demo

This interactive AI demo performs high-speed handwritten digit recognition entirely inside your browser! Instead of sending your drawings to a remote server, it uses ONNX Runtime Web (built by Microsoft) to execute a pre-trained neural network locally.

Under the hood, this is powered by WebAssembly (WASM). The heavy neural network math is compiled from C++ into WASM, allowing your browser to crunch numbers at near-native CPU speeds for absolute privacy and zero latency.

The pre-trained MNIST digit recognition model powering this demo was trained and provided by the official open-source ONNX Model Zoo (mnist-8.onnx) (maintained by the ONNX community and Microsoft).
